Method, automatic updates and sources

What the probability means

For the same Beijing-time 08:00–08:00 window, count the complete ensemble members whose forecast precipitation reaches 25 mm. This is an uncalibrated ensemble member fraction. It is not the probability of flooding at a shop or stock damage, and it must not be replaced by the historical 67.9% example.

What the cost means

Relocate: relocation cost + probability × conditional loss after relocation. Keep in place: probability × conditional loss if kept. Loss is an average conditional estimate for this rain event, not the full value of the stock. The model cannot determine your true loss.

Worked hypothetical example

At 60%, relocation cost CNY 80, conditional loss if kept CNY 1,000 and conditional loss after relocation CNY 100, expected costs are CNY 600 and CNY 140. CNY 540 is a tie; CNY 600 means keep in place. These amounts are hypothetical.

Automatic updates

Fetches when opened and checks hourly while this page stays open. With the local service, the background worker can check hourly after the page closes; the computer and service must remain on.

Weather older than 3 hours or an incomplete selected window pauses the suggestion. A failed update keeps old data and marks the status; missing data is never treated as no rain. A daily reference check does not alter the frozen experiment.
