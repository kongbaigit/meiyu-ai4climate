- Title: Hangzhou Municipal Government General Office notice issuing the Hangzhou urban flood and typhoon emergency plan (PDF)

## Conservative adaptation ideas for small shops

1. **Sort moisture-sensitive stock.** Identify paper goods, textiles, and packaged dry goods; keep them off the floor, away from doors and visible seepage, and ventilated. Do not handle wet electrical equipment. This is a conservative adaptation from the sources above, not a promised loss reduction.
2. **Check what is already prepared.** List existing rainwear, lights, cleaning/absorbent supplies, and packaging by name, quantity, specification, and condition; identify gaps before buying. Government reserve rules do not create a small-shop purchase duty.
3. **Follow official weather channels.** Check local meteorological and government warnings before opening and before heavy rain. Use Meiyu Prepare only for preparation-cost explanation; it cannot replace an official warning, evacuation, or safety instruction.
