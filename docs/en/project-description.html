Problem and intended users

Meiyu Prepare is for Hangzhou small shops that may need to sort or relocate moisture-sensitive stock before a heavy-rain scenario. Public market monitoring describes effects of prolonged rain on product quality, harvesting, storage, and transport, but there is no evidence yet that a particular shop needs this prototype, would use it, or would pay for it. Those assumptions remain to be tested with merchants.

The climate value is adaptation and preparedness. Two years of one-station data cannot attribute climate change or establish a long-term trend. Airport precipitation is not shop flooding, and the prototype does not issue official warnings.

Method and result

The AI task is to map public numerical-weather inputs to the probability that nominal UTC-day reference precipitation reaches 25 mm. This is a research threshold, not an official warning level. A small logistic regression (logistic_rain, C=10) was selected only on the validation segment. The language model does not generate observations or labels.

Product and data boundary

The English main app (/en/) provides the stock-relocation decision and automatically requests the public Hangzhou Ensemble. The English static historical demo (/en/demo.html) keeps the historical evidence offline and its Latest weather & completeness view requests Ensemble only after the user clicks. The original Python/Chinese research workbench (app.py) remains a separate button-triggered Forecast workbench.

The English pages show numerical forecast evidence, archived ERA5 explanatory context, and archived SSOD reference completeness as separate concepts. They do not claim a complete real-time station-and-forecast implementation. Where the English Ensemble view is available, it shows seven complete future UTC-day windows for Hangzhou or Ningbo, with retrieval time, source, and completeness; an incomplete or stale result pauses the current conclusion. These paths and the original workbench must not be conflated.

Decision example and validation path

Decision-cost example

Let p be the scenario event estimate, C the relocation cost, and L0 and L1 the conditional average losses without and with relocation.

Ekeep = pL0     Erelocate = C + pL1

The expected difference is p(L0 - L1) - C. A positive value favors Relocate, a negative value favors Keep in place, and equality displays a tie. The browser compares unrounded CNY values and displays two decimals.

Hypothetical 60% example. With relocation cost C=CNY 80, conditional losses L0=CNY 1,000 and L1=CNY 100, totals are CNY 600 for Keep in place and CNY 140 for Relocate, difference CNY 460. Tie cost is C=CNY 540; at C=CNY 600, choose Keep in place. These are scenario figures only.
