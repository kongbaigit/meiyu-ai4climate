## Invitation draft

Hello. We are testing whether the Meiyu Prepare page is easy to understand and would like to invite you to try a historical rainfall prototype for about 10 minutes. You will follow a few tasks and explain what you understand. No weather or technical knowledge is required, and we do not need your shop address, turnover, or inventory information.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question or stop at any time. We do not record audio or video by default; we use an anonymous code for operation notes and necessary feedback. This uses historical data and does not ask you to act on real weather. We want to hear what is confusing, difficult, or not worth using.

If you are willing, we will confirm a time and a way to open the demo.

## Host consent text

“We are testing the page, not your ability. It will take about 10 minutes and uses historical rainfall and hypothetical cost, not current-weather advice. You may skip a question, pause, or stop without giving a reason and without disadvantage.

We do not record audio or video by default. We use an anonymous code for operation time, help needed, and necessary feedback; we do not collect your name, shop address, or financial information. The record custodian is ____; private storage is ____; raw records will be deleted by ____. We report only de-identified aggregates. If we want to quote your words, we will ask separately.

Your code is ____. To withdraw the record, contact ____ through today's channel before ____. A de-identified aggregate may no longer be separable after the deadline, so we will not publish this round's aggregate before then.

Do you agree to participate in this way?”

Fill every blank before the session. The withdrawal date must allow a real request and precede raw-record deletion. If there is no consent, end the session and record only an anonymous refusal count; never record the refuser's identity or reason.

## Separate consent for quotes

Default to a de-identified meaning summary. If a verbatim quote is needed, ask: “May we record this sentence and use it in a project report after removing identity details? Refusing will not affect participation.” Record verbatim only after explicit consent, and show the proposed quote again before closing. Consent to participate is not consent to publish a quote. Remove place, shop, and name details; if it cannot be de-identified, do not quote.

## Recruitment record

Target 5-8 people, preferably people with stock, moisture, or rain-preparation experience. Use existing private channels and do not publicly collect personal details. Record invitation, consent, refusal, and no-response counts; do not publish a contact list linked to test IDs.

Teammates use R IDs and formal participants use P IDs. Non-target people can reveal interface problems but cannot establish Hangzhou small-shop demand. If fewer than five people join, report the shortfall; do not add rehearsals to reach the target.
