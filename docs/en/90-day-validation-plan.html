90-Day Validation Plan

A plan for understanding, operation, and forecast availability - not a completed merchant pilot.

Scope. The initial user hypothesis is a Hangzhou small shop that may need moisture and rain preparation. Recruiters, shop types, dates, and team contacts are not confirmed. If work starts in late October 2026, the 90-day window ends around late January 2027 and does not include a complete next plum-rain season.

Pre-specified exploratory targets

Operation

At least 80% independently complete window selection, probability interpretation, source lookup, and reference reveal within 180 seconds.

Understanding

At least 80% explain that low probability is not safety, rainfall is not flooding, and cost is a hypothetical scenario.

Technology

Record source, retrieval time, publication/availability evidence, parsing, and missingness. Unverified availability is not a real decision replay.

Model

Keep event and scoring rules fixed in new independent data and compare with an equivalent baseline. Small samples are reported as insufficient.

Cost

Record team-observed run time and API calls separately. Merchant preparation cost and loss-avoidance ability need separate evidence.

Stop rules, privacy, and reporting

Stop and narrow conditions

  • Stop the corresponding effect claim if same-station, same-window, interpretable-quality data cannot be obtained.
  • If real forecast availability cannot be established, retain the offline demo only.
  • If participants repeatedly treat probability as an official warning or flood result, stop outward action guidance and revise wording first.
  • If the method does not beat an interpretable baseline, retain the baseline and narrow the claim that AI is necessary.

Participation and privacy

No back-office connection or on-site device is required. Recruit volunteers, explain purpose and withdrawal, and record only completion and necessary feedback. Store address, turnover, inventory value, and personal contact details are not default fields. The public prototype shows no private merchant information.

The prepared execution package is UT-20261016-v1. It contains recruitment text, host script, fixed tasks, scoring rules, blank individual records, and an aggregation template. No real-user test has been conducted.

Fixed denominator rules

A person who consents, starts the task, and allows record retention enters denominator N. Full withdrawal is deleted and excluded. A technical interruption that is not withdrawn remains in N and is reported separately. Independent task success and first-attempt comprehension have separate numerators and denominators. Help, retries, and post-explanation answers do not count as first-attempt success.

Reporting template

For each round, report version, dates, invitation and consent counts, retained N, withdrawals, technical interruptions, completion count, first-attempt comprehension count, time range and median for independent successes, common misunderstandings, changes, and the next decision. If N=0, write not conducted / no records to aggregate, not 0% or 100%.

Interpretation. The targets are exploratory. A small sample cannot support population adoption, payment, avoided-loss, carbon, or weather-effect claims. New-season forecast performance requires a separate independent seasonal evaluation.
